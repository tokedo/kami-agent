# Harvesting

A kami placed at a harvesting node accrues MUSU over time. Harvesting
drains health; resting restores it.

MUSU accrual depends on the node and on the kami's stats. Collecting
banks the accrued MUSU to the account.

A kami at low health can be liquidated by another player, which is how
MUSU changes hands outside trade.
